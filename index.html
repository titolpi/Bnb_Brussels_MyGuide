✈️ Airports & Getting Here
▼

How to reach the apartment from Brussels airports:

🛫 Brussels National Zaventem Airport (BRU)

• Direct Train: Takes ~20–30 mins. Return tickets cost ~€20–€23 (cheaper on weekends).

• Uber / Bolt: ~€35–€50 one-way, ~25–45 mins depending on traffic.

🛬 Brussels South Charleroi Airport (CRL)

• Flibco Shuttle Bus (Strongly Recommended): Direct to Gare du Midi. Costs ~€16 to max €20 one-way. Departs every 20 mins, takes ~50–60 mins. [Book Flibco Bus ↗]

• Uber / Bolt: ~€90–€130 one-way.

• Pre-booked Private Shuttle: ~€70–€100. Useful options to pre-book: [Transfers2Airport ↗] or [GetTransfer ↗].

🚆 Getting Around & Trains
▼

Local City Transport

• Walking: Easy 15-minute walk to city center.
• Tram: Lemonnier stop is 400m away (a few steps, no lifts). Direct 2 stops to center.
• Payment: Tap your bank card directly at validators (automatically caps daily rate). For MOBIB cards, buy at Gare du Midi KIOSK (-1 level).

Train Day Trips (SNCB)

Buy tickets via SNCB website or the SNCB app (iOS App Store / Android Play Store). Weekend return tickets are cheaper! Watch out for IC (express) vs L (slow local) trains.

🔒 Closing & Locking Flat Door
▼

To properly close main glass sliding door before locking:

  1. Pull handle entirely down and slide door shut.
  2. Lift handle completely back up while pulling door towards you.
  3. Listen for a light "click" indicating door is sealed.

Once clicked, lock securely with key.
